──────────────────────────────────────────────
1. PATTERN MECHANICS
──────────────────────────────────────────────

*.log:
  A bare glob with no leading slash and no trailing slash.
  Per gitignore(5) §PATTERN FORMAT, it matches the filename component
  at every directory depth, covering both regular files and directories
  named *.log.

panic*.log.*:
  Matches Cadence Virtuoso panic log files that have an additional suffix
  after ".log." (e.g. panic.log.1, panic_core.log.backup). The trailing .*
  requires at least one more extension segment, so plain "panic.log" is NOT
  matched by this specific rule; it is, however, matched by the broader *.log
  rule earlier in the file.

*.cdslck*:
  Matches Cadence Design System OpenAccess database lock files. The trailing *
  allows additional characters after .cdslck (e.g. .cdslck, .cdslck1,
  .cdslck.tmp). The leading * makes it a bare glob matching at any depth.
