1. PATTERN MECHANICS
──────────────────────────────────────────────

All three rules are bare globs with no leading slash and no trailing slash.
Per gitignore(5) §PATTERN FORMAT:

  - A pattern without a slash is matched against the filename component at
    every directory depth.
  - Without a trailing slash, the pattern matches both regular files and
    directories of the same name.
  - Files inside a matched directory are implicitly ignored (Git stops
    descending into ignored directories).
